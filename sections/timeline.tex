\section{Timeline}
\label{sec:timeline}

For the remaining of this program, I will focus primarily on fabricating, testing, and assembling the independent converters circuit for full power autonomous operation. At the same time, I will draft the written dissertation and prepare for the oral defense. I am expected to complete the first written draft by late July and defend by mid August. The completion of this thesis will affirm that autonomous flying robots powered by soft artificial muscle can be realized, and they are ready for more practical applications.
